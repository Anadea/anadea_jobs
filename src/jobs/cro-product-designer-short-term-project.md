**About the Role**\
We’re looking for a Product Designer specialized in conversion rate optimization to design\
paywalls, onboarding flows and subscription experiences across several apps in the\
company’s portfolio. You’ll work directly with Product Owners and cross-functional teams to\
design, test and iterate on experiences that maximize conversion without sacrificing the user\
experience.\
\
**Role Responsibilities**\
● Design in Figma and set up/upload paywalls and subscription flows in Superwall\
and RevenueCat.\
● Design and optimize Web-to-Web (W2W) and Web-to-App (W2A) funnels to drive\
acquisition and conversion.\
● Collaborate with multiple Product Owners in parallel, managing several projects at once.\
● Design and maintain design systems and component libraries in Figma.\
● Analyze test results and propose data-driven iterations.\
● Ensure designs adapt across different devices, languages and screen sizes.\
● Document processes and keep the team’s workflow up to date.\
● Explore and apply AI-assisted workflows to speed up research, competitive\
benchmarking and the generation of design variants.\
\
**Key requirements**\
● Senior experience in digital product design, ideally in subscription “products” to make it\
broader (mobile-first).\
● Portfolio with CRO, paywall or onboarding case studies.\
● Strong command of Figma (design systems, components, prototyping).\
● Experience with Superwall and/or RevenueCat for paywall implementation.\
● Ability to work with multiple stakeholders/POs at the same time.\
● English B1+ level is required\
● Experience in using AI tools to accelerate UX/UI workflows is mandatory (Figma AI,\
Claude, ChatGPT, or AI prototyping tools).\
● Experience with A/B testing and product analytics tools.\
\
**Preferred Experience**\
● Interest or experience in building custom AI agents or automated workflows that\
support the design process (briefing, documentation, conversion-pattern analysis).\
● Motion & animation skills — able to bring designs to life with tools like After\
Effects and/or Lottie (animated paywalls, onboarding transitions and UI\
micro-interactions).\
● Basic knowledge of conversion-oriented copywriting.\
● Experience in subscription products across different niches (utilities, productivity,\
health&fitness, photo/video tools, AI consumer products).\
\
**Other requirements**\
● Experience working in a fast-paced, entrepreneurial team environment.\
● Ability to respond quickly to changing priorities.\
● Excellent communication skills, highly organized and with sharp attention to detail.\
● Committed to delivering excellent work at every step of the process.\
● Brings a positive, respectful and emotionally intelligent attitude, cares about the\
people around them and helps build a healthy, collaborative team culture.
